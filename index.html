About Me

I am a data professional with a Master’s in Data Analytics (Distinction) from Aston University, where I specialized in statistical modeling and machine learning. My research journey—from analyzing superconductivity through stochastic ML to designing secure cloud architectures—has instilled a dual focus: technical rigor in building systems and critical inquiry into their societal implications.

Currently, I am working toward becoming a Data Scientist/Engineer, leveraging tools like Python, SQL, and TensorFlow to design scalable data pipelines and ML models. My long-term vision is to contribute to advancing machine learning, particularly in stochastic modeling and neural networks, while ensuring these technologies serve equitable outcomes.

Research & Industry Perspective
  • Technical Depth: Built ETL systems (40% efficiency gains), time-series forecasting models (RNNs), and NLP applications, grounded in coursework like Probabilistic Modeling and Statistical ML.
